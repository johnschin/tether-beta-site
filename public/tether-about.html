Why Tether exists.

Most organizational change fails — not because leaders chose the wrong strategy, but because they underinvested in the human side of change. The research is consistent: 70% of change initiatives fall short of their goals, and the gap almost always lives in individual adoption.

We built Tether because we kept seeing the same pattern in our work with organizations: the framework was solid, the communications were clear, the training was adequate — and people were still stuck. Anxious. Resistant. Not because they were obstinate, but because no one had helped them move through their own internal journey.

Tether is our attempt to fix that at scale. To give every employee — not just the ones who seek out coaching or flag themselves to HR — access to practical, in-the-moment support when they're navigating change.

The framework underneath Tether.

ADKAR-Aligned

Tether's coaching logic maps directly to the Prosci ADKAR model — the most widely used change management framework in the world. Each conversation is designed to understand where an employee is in the ADKAR journey and provide the support that stage actually requires.

Evidence-Based

Tether draws on CBT, ACT, motivational interviewing, and positive psychology. These aren't wellness buzzwords — they're the methodologies that have been validated in organizational and clinical settings for decades.

Boundary-Clear

Tether is a coaching and development tool, not a clinical service. It knows its limits. When an employee's situation requires more than coaching, Tether redirects them — to their EAP, to HR, or to emergency services if needed.
