About the product

Tether is an AI-powered coaching tool for employees and managers navigating organizational change. It provides private, in-the-moment coaching grounded in ADKAR, CBT, and motivational interviewing — available anytime, not just during business hours or in scheduled training sessions.
Your EAP is reactive — someone typically needs to be struggling before they're referred. Tether is proactive. It meets employees before they disengage, while the change is still unfolding. Tether also doesn't provide clinical care. It's a coaching tool for navigating work transitions, not a mental health service.
No. Tether is a coaching and development tool. It is not therapy, counseling, medical care, or a crisis service. If an employee's situation requires clinical support, Tether will redirect them to appropriate resources.
Reorgs. New leadership. Role changes. Technology rollouts. Process shifts. Mergers. Team restructuring. Anything that disrupts how people work and creates uncertainty about what comes next.

Privacy and data

Your employer sees anonymized, aggregate data only — no individual conversation transcripts, no session-level attribution, no way to identify which employee said what. Your coaching conversations are private.
No one, by default. Conversation content is not shared with your employer, manager, or HR team. Tether's safety protocols may log certain crisis-related events (see the Crisis Escalation Protocol), but individual conversations remain private.
Summaries of session conversations are stored to augment individual user's progress and to reinforce learning new skills over time. Summaries are stored until the user's authorization expires. See the Privacy Policy for full details on data practices.
Tether is not a covered entity under HIPAA — it is a coaching tool, not a healthcare provider. However, it is built with strong data security practices including HTTPS encryption, row-level database security, and minimal data collection. See the Privacy Policy and Privacy & Trust statement.
