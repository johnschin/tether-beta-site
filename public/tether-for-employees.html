For employees

You didn't ask for this change. Tether helps you move through it anyway.

A private, in-the-moment coaching tool — for the reorg, the new manager, the system that doesn't make sense yet.

The gap

Most change programs talk to the org. Not to you.

Change announcements explain the strategy. They don't explain what it means for your specific role, your team dynamic, or the forty things you actually do before noon. Town halls answer the questions leadership is prepared for — not the ones you're lying awake thinking about at 11pm.

Tether is different. It's not a corporate rollout tool. It's not a compliance training dressed up with a chatbot interface. It's a private coaching space — built for you to process what's actually happening, work through what's uncertain, and figure out your next step. No performance implications. No manager visibility. Just a clear-headed place to think.

What you get

What Tether gives you

Privacy

A private space to process.

Tether is confidential. What you share doesn't go to your manager or HR. You can be honest — about your confusion, your frustration, your fear — without it affecting how you're perceived at work.

Relevance

Coaching for your actual situation.

Not generic advice. Not breathing exercises. Practical support for the specific change you're navigating — the reorg announcement that just landed, the manager who started last week, the process that keeps changing.

Availability

Steady support when the timing is bad.

Change doesn't hit during business hours. Tether is available when the announcement drops at 10pm, or when you're lying awake at 2am trying to figure out what comes next and whether your role still makes sense.

"Adoption happens inside individuals. Tether makes that invisible journey a little less lonely."

In practice

What a conversation with Tether looks like.

You open Tether and say what's actually on your mind. Tether listens, reflects it back accurately, and asks one good question that helps you think more clearly. Then it offers one practical next step — not ten.

This isn't therapy. It isn't a generic chatbot looping you through canned responses. It's structured coaching, grounded in evidence-based psychology, available the moment you need it.

Scenario

The reorg just got announced.

You don't know if your role still exists in the same form. Tether helps you separate what you actually know from what you're assuming, and identify the one question worth asking your manager tomorrow.

Scenario

You have a new manager.

The relationship feels uncertain and you're recalibrating everything. Tether helps you understand what the adjustment period normally looks like — and how to build trust with someone new without losing your footing in the process.

Scenario

The system everyone hates just went live.

You're slower, more frustrated, less confident than you were last month. Tether normalizes the learning curve and helps you figure out where to focus your energy first — instead of spinning in all directions.

Clarity

A few things Tether isn't.

Tether is not therapy. It's a coaching tool for navigating organizational change — built on evidence-based psychology but not a substitute for clinical mental health care. If you're experiencing serious mental health distress, please reach out to your EAP or a licensed professional. They're the right resource for that, and using them is exactly what they're there for.

Tether is also not a performance tool. Your employer receives only anonymized, aggregate data about how change is landing across the organization — never your individual conversations, never your identity linked to any specific response. What you say to Tether stays between you and Tether.
